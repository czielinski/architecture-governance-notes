# Architecture decision record

## Decision summary
- Title / ID:
- Status: proposed / accepted / superseded / withdrawn
- Accountable decision owner:
- Author and consulted roles:
- Decision date and next review trigger:

## Problem and scope
What needs to be decided, for which services or teams, and by when? What happens if no change is made?

## Constraints and assumptions
List the constraints that are fixed and the assumptions that may change. Include regulatory, commercial, operational and integration dependencies where relevant. Avoid personal data or confidential figures in public copies.

## Options considered
For each viable option, including retaining the current approach where relevant, record:
- What it enables and what remains unresolved.
- Change effort and recurring operating effort.
- Risks, dependencies and exit implications.
- Evidence, uncertainty and who supplied the information.

## Recommendation and decision
Explain the trade-off. Record the recommendation separately from the authorised decision and its owner. Capture material dissent and any conditions of approval.

## Delivery and operating responsibilities
Who implements the change, owns the resulting service, accepts it into operation and funds ongoing work? Identify hand-offs that still need agreement.

## Follow-up
- Action / owner / due date / evidence of completion.
- Conditions that require review or escalation.
- Link to a superseding decision if circumstances change.

## Outcome review
After implementation, compare the observed result with the original assumptions. Record what is known, what is still uncertain and whether a further decision is needed.
