# Example: when to replace a service platform

**Fictional example for using the template. No company, cost estimate or outcome below represents an employer.**

## Problem
A service platform contract expires before a group-wide platform strategy will be decided. The team must choose between renewal and replacement.

## Options
1. Replace immediately with the platform used by another group entity. This may improve future compatibility but requires a migration before the target scope is known.
2. Negotiate a limited renewal. This preserves time to clarify the group strategy but prolongs the current platform’s operating burden.
3. Allow the contract to expire without a replacement. This fails the service-continuity requirement and is not viable.

## Assumptions
A supported renewal is commercially available. The existing platform can meet mandatory service requirements during the renewal period. Neither assumption is treated as verified until its owner provides evidence.

## Recommendation
Pursue a limited renewal, conditional on support, commercial terms and a funded plan to resolve the target-platform question. Compare the total cost of the interim arrangement with an immediate change before final approval.

## Decision owner
The accountable IT executive decides after input from the service owner, procurement, finance, architecture and security. The architect authors the recommendation but does not acquire budget authority through the document.

## Review triggers
Revisit the decision when the group strategy is agreed, renewal terms become unacceptable, or support and service requirements can no longer be met.

## Follow-up
Assign named owners and dates for the commercial negotiation, requirements check and target-platform decision. Record the authorised decision and its conditions before proceeding.
